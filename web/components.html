ascii-wall

Embed any ASCIIWall scene on your site, your GitHub Pages portfolio or your docs. It's one script tag and one element. Every scene is live and reacts to the cursor or touch, and it pauses itself while it's off-screen.

Use it

<script src="https://YOUR_USER.github.io/ASCIIWall/dist/asciiwall.js"></script>

<ascii-wall scene="tide" style="height:360px"></ascii-wall>

<!-- options -->
<ascii-wall scene="hearth" density="1.4" glow="1.2" tint="amber" fps="30"></ascii-wall>
<ascii-wall scene="garden" contributions="data/contributions.json"></ascii-wall>
